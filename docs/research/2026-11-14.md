## 2. Specs / upstream behaviour
- Linux e1000e, [2026 log](https://git.zx2c4.com/wireguard-linux/log/drivers/net/ethernet/intel/e1000e): "clear DPG_EN after reset to avoid autonomous power-gating" (2026-02-25), a DMA error-cleanup leak fix (2026-03-10). The first may matter to PCH parts: filed as an issue (below).
- Proxmox kernel changelog (2026-11-02, unconfirmed upstream): I219 TSO corruption/hang erratum workaround. The driver uses no TSO or offloads, so not affected.
- [RX stops after link down/up on 8086:550a](https://lkml.iu.edu/hypermail/linux/kernel/2601.1/12436.html) (Jan 2026, fixed in mainline by 3c7bf5af2196). Driver never resets the PHY; a link-flap test on a PCH part would still be sensible.
